# Personas

Synthetic personas for the audible level harness, one per customer segment. Convert each into `personas/<id>.json` using the Persona schema in the design spec. These are hypotheses, not research.

| ID | Segment | Name, age | Location | Behaviors | Values | Job hypothesis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| p01 | Visually impaired DIYers | Marcus Reed, 44 | Columbus, Ohio | Lost most of his sight to glaucoma at 38. Hangs shelves and fixes things using a beeping phone app and touch.
